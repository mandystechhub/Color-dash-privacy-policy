Privacy Policy

Color Dash

Last Updated: 26 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Color Dash handles information when you use the mobile game.

Color Dash is designed as an offline, single-player colour-matching arcade game. This policy applies to the Color Dash mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Color Dash is designed not to collect, request, or transmit personal information to the developer.

The game may store gameplay information, such as your current score and highest score, locally on your device to help you track your progress.

This locally stored gameplay information is not transmitted to the developer.

3. Data Sharing

Color Dash does not share personal information with the developer or third parties, provided the published version contains no advertising networks, analytics tools, tracking technologies, or other third-party components that collect or transmit user data.

4. Internet Access and Permissions

Color Dash is designed to work offline and should not require an internet connection or unnecessary device permissions.

The application's actual permissions and network behaviour depend on the components included in the published version.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Gameplay information saved by Color Dash is stored locally on your device.

You can delete this information by clearing the application's data through your device settings or uninstalling the application. If the game provides an in-game reset option, you may also use that option to reset your gameplay records.

The developer does not maintain a server containing your gameplay records.

6. Children's Privacy

Color Dash does not intentionally request personal information from players.

If the game is directed toward children or is included in a children's audience category, the developer will ensure that the application and its distribution comply with applicable children's privacy laws and relevant Google Play Families policies.

7. Third-Party Services

This Privacy Policy assumes that Color Dash does not include third-party advertising services, analytics tools, tracking SDKs, or other components that collect or transmit user data.

If third-party services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant information practices.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game or its information practices change. Any updates will be published with a revised last-updated date.
